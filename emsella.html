Emsella Pelvic Floor Therapy

Non-Invasive Pelvic Floor Strengthening

Rebuild Pelvic Floor Strength — Fully Clothed, Zero Downtime

Emsella uses high-intensity electromagnetic (HIFEM) technology to induce deep, repetitive contractions of the entire pelvic floor — far more than a patient can achieve through voluntary Kegel exercises alone.

Patients simply sit, fully clothed, on the Emsella chair for a 30-minute session. It is a popular option for men and women dealing with stress or urge urinary incontinence, postpartum pelvic floor weakness, or general core stability concerns, and requires no recovery time.
