    Cosmetic Botox Services

    Physician-Administered Injectables for Natural, Youthful, and Refreshed Facial Aesthetics.

    The #1 Treatment of Its Kind

    Look Naturally Refreshed, Not Frozen

    Botox is a purified protein produced in a controlled medical setting to safely relax targeted muscles that cause repetitive dynamic wrinkles. It is the first and only FDA-approved treatment to temporarily improve the appearance of moderate to severe forehead lines, crow's feet, and glabellar frown lines in adults.

    Under the expert clinical supervision of Dr. Jan Gillespie, your treatment is precisely mapped to your facial anatomy, ensuring natural animation and smooth, refreshed skin.

    Advantages of Botox at NoCo MedSpa

    Safe, rapid, and physician-supervised aesthetic care.

    Reduces Wrinkles & Fine Lines

    Most commonly celebrated for diminishing frown lines between the eyebrows, horizontal forehead creases, and crow's feet around the eyes.

    Non-Surgical Solution

    Unlike invasive surgical facelifts or brow lifts, Botox involves quick, microscopic injections with virtually zero discomfort or surgical recovery.

    We tailor exact unit measurements to your unique facial musculature, ensuring symmetric and aesthetically balanced outcomes.

    Clinical Transparency

    Important Safety Information & Approved Uses

    BOTOX® Cosmetic (onabotulinumtoxinA) is an FDA-approved prescription medicine that is injected into muscles and used to temporarily improve the look of moderate to severe forehead lines, crow's feet lines, and frown lines between the eyebrows in adults.

    BOTOX® Cosmetic dosing units are not interchangeable with any other botulinum toxin product. At NoCo MedSpa, all neurotoxin treatments are administered or supervised by board-certified physician Dr. Jan Gillespie according to strict medical protocols.
