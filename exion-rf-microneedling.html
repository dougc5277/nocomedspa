Exion RF Microneedling

Combines fine-needle microneedling with radiofrequency energy and AI-based energy delivery to boost collagen and elastin production.

Exion RF Microneedling improves skin texture and tone, softens fine lines, tightens skin, minimizes acne scarring and pore size, and increases natural hyaluronic acid production. Initial improvement is visible within a few weeks, with full results developing over 2–3 months. Expect mild redness, swelling, or pinpoint bleeding that resolves within a few days; most patients resume normal activity in 24–48 hours.

Treatable areas: full face, neck, chest, scars. Recommended course: 3 treatments, every 4 weeks.
