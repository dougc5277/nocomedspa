How EMSCULPT neo Works

Two therapies in a single applicator for unparalleled efficacy.

1. Radiofrequency (RF) Heating

Within less than 4 minutes, the temperature in subcutaneous fat reaches levels that cause apoptosis (permanent cell death). Damaged fat cells are slowly and naturally flushed from the body through the lymphatic system over the following weeks.

2. HIFEM+ High-Intensity Magnetic Energy

HIFEM+ energy contracts 100% of muscle fibers in the target area at intensities not achievable during typical gym workouts. Extreme stress forces the muscle to adapt, resulting in increased muscle fibers and cells.

3. Treatment Protocol

A standard protocol consists of four 30-minute treatment sessions scheduled 5–10 days apart. Peak clinical results appear within 2–4 weeks after the final session.

What does an EMSCULPT neo treatment feel like?

How fast will I see results?
You begin to feel tangible results right after the treatments. Positive results are usually reported two to four weeks after the last session and continue to improve for several weeks following the treatments.
Is there any downtime after EMSCULPT neo?
There is absolutely zero downtime with EMSCULPT neo. You can resume your daily routine, exercise, and work immediately after each 30-minute session.
Am I a good candidate for EMSCULPT neo?
Almost anyone can benefit from the EMSCULPT neo procedure. It is FDA-cleared for patients with a BMI up to 35. During your consultation, Dr. Jan Gillespie will evaluate your medical history and aesthetic goals to confirm your candidacy.
