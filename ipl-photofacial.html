Sun Damage, Redness & Rosacea

IPL Photofacial

Intense Pulsed Light (IPL) — also known as a Photo Facial — treats visible brown spots caused by sun exposure, broken capillaries, redness, and flushing associated with rosacea.

IPL is available for the full face, neck, chest, hands, and lower arms, making it a versatile option for sun-damaged skin anywhere on the body.

Sessions run 45–60 minutes. Recommended course: 2 treatments — face/neck every 4 weeks, chest/hands/arms every 5–6 weeks.
