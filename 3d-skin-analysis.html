    Advanced 3D Skin Analysis with AURA

    A Personalized, Data-Driven Evaluation of Your Skin — Experience This New Way of Seeing Your Skin in True 3D.

    Meet Your Digital Twin

    Advanced Skin Consultations in True 3D

    AURA is an advanced 3D imaging system that captures a photo-realistic digital twin of your face and neck in a single high-resolution scan.

    Using multiple calibrated cameras and synchronized lighting units, AURA generates a comprehensive 3D model that your provider can rotate, zoom, and examine from any angle during your consultation.

    The system meticulously analyzes wrinkles, pores, texture, redness, brown spots, facial proportions, and volume changes with clinical precision. This helps our medical team identify concerns before they surface, explain findings clearly, and formulate a treatment protocol tailored to your actual anatomy.
