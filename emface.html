    EMFACE: Needle-Free Facial Toning & Lifting

    The First & Only Non-Invasive Procedure that Simultaneously Treats Facial Muscles and Skin to Lift, Sculpt, and Smooth.

    Dual Breakthrough Technology

    Restore Your Facial Foundation Without Needles

    Facial aging is not just skin deep — it occurs when underlying facial elevator muscles lose tone and the skin loses collagen and elastin.

    Developed by BTL Aesthetics, EMFACE is the only non-invasive facial treatment that targets the structural muscular framework of your face. By combining High-Intensity Facial Electrical Stimulation (HIFES) with Synchronized Radiofrequency (Sync RF), EMFACE stimulates 75,000 targeted muscle contractions while simultaneously remodeling collagen in the dermis and SMAS layer.

    The result is a visible natural lift in the brows, cheeks, jawline, and double chin area — with zero needles, zero bruising, and zero downtime.

    The Science Behind EMFACE

    Two Synergistic Energies in a Single 20-Minute Session

    Comfortable, needle-free applicators placed on the forehead, cheeks, or submentum.

    Energy 01

    HIFES Muscle Stimulation

    High-Intensity Facial Electrical Stimulation selectively recruits and contracts the delicate facial elevator muscles.

    • ✓ 75,000 Contractions: Generates supramaximal facial pulses that are impossible to achieve voluntarily.
    • ✓ Targets Elevator Muscles: Treats the frontalis (elevates brow) and zygomaticus major, minor, and risorius (lifts midface).
    • ✓ Restores Density & Tone: Thickens muscle fibers to re-establish the structural anchor of your facial tissues.
    Energy 02

    Synchronized Radiofrequency

    Simultaneously heats the dermis and SMAS layer to an optimal 40–42°C within minutes of treatment without affecting subcutaneous facial fat.

    • ✓ Collagen & Elastin Remodeling: Stimulates structural proteins to smooth wrinkles, fine lines, and laxity.
    • ✓ Tones the SMAS: Firms the superficial musculoaponeurotic system — the exact layer targeted in surgical facelifts.
    • ✓ Comfortable Warmth: Feels like a warm facial stone massage with zero burning sensation.
    Targeted Neck & Chin Sculpting

    EMFACE Submentum: Jawline Definition & Double Chin Reduction

    Tackle fullness under the chin and restore a sharp, defined jawline without surgery or painful fat-dissolving injections.

    The EMFACE Submentum applicator delivers synchronized RF and electrical stimulation specifically calibrated for the submental area, tightening loose skin, reducing submental fullness, and strengthening the digastric muscular foundation under the chin.
