Understanding The Process

What Is a Chemical Peel?

A chemical peel is a professional skin-resurfacing treatment designed to gently exfoliate the outer layers of the skin and reveal a fresher, more radiant appearance. It uses carefully selected clinical solutions to exfoliate the skin and encourage the natural renewal process.

Minimal Downtime

Light Peels

Gently dissolves the outermost layer of dead skin cells (epidermis). Ideal for mild dullness, skin texture refinement, and maintenance as part of a regular skincare regimen with virtually no social downtime.

Moderate Correction

Medium Peels

Penetrates into the upper middle layers of the dermis. Targets noticeable sun damage, pigment irregularities, age spots, and superficial fine lines. Involves light flaking over 3 to 5 days.

Intensive Transformation

Deeper Resurfacing Peels

Provides significant corrective results for pronounced photodamage, deeper wrinkles, and persistent hyperpigmentation. Requires structured aftercare and a short healing window for maximum renewal.

Visible Transformation

Why Clients Love Chemical Peels

Chemical peels are among our most popular clinical treatments because they can address several common skin concerns simultaneously:

  • ✓
    Dull or rough-looking skin — restores radiant glow
  • ✓
    Uneven tone and texture — creates silky surface smoothness
  • ✓
    Fine lines and early signs of aging — softens facial creases
  • ✓
    Sun spots and visible photodamage — diminishes UV damage
  • ✓
    Mild acne and congested pores — clears debris and refines pores
  • ✓
    Hyperpigmentation and discoloration — evens complexion
  • ✓
    Superficial scarring and post-breakout marks

Bonus Benefit: As older, damaged surface cells shed, the skin underneath looks smoother and more luminous. Clients consistently find that their at-home skincare products penetrate significantly better and apply more evenly.

Formulations Tailored to You

Which Peel is Right for You?

There is no one-size-fits-all peel. During your consultation, your provider will evaluate your skin, review your goals, and recommend the best option for your complexion and comfort level.

Radiance & Glow

Glyco-Blend

A potent glycolic acid blend designed to dissolve intercellular cement, polish away dull surface buildup, and reveal immediate luminosity.

Gentle Hydration

Lactic Acid 55%

An ultra-hydrating alpha-hydroxy acid peel ideal for sensitive or dry skin types seeking gentle exfoliation, moisture retention, and subtle brightening.

Clarity & Acne

Jessner’s Peel

The classic synergistic blend of salicylic acid, lactic acid, and resorcinol. Excellent for clarifying oily, congested skin, active breakouts, and rough texture.

Mild Refinement

Light Jessner’s

A milder adaptation of the Jessner’s formula designed for gentle pore refinement and smoothing with minimal to no visible peeling.

Pigment Brightening

Light Jessner’s Bright

Infused with targeted brightening botanicals and tyrosinase inhibitors to specifically treat stubborn melasma, dark spots, and sun freckles.

Deep Resurfacing

TCA 15% Formulations

Trichloroacetic acid formulation designed for deeper correction of fine lines, etched wrinkles, actinic damage, and post-inflammatory pigmentation.
